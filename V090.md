# v0.9 — continuous t0s + timing-uncertainty output

**v0.9 = the certified v0.8.9 chain + stage 6 (`--subtick`).** Launcher:
`run_v09.sh <FLOW.hdf5> <out_dir> [n_workers=8] [n_gpus=4]`.

## What stage 6 adds on top of v0.8.9

1. **Continuous t0s.** After every assignment stage is final, hits are
   grouped by their exact final t0; each group's light is re-predicted
   from scratch as ONE bag of hits (a single batched perceiver call for
   all groups — the interaction-level form validated by the v097
   superposition test), and the group time is refined on a 0.25-tick
   grid (±3 ticks) followed by the analytic quadratic minimum (9-point
   ±1-tick window, vertex acceptance ±0.75 — measured better than the
   2x2's 7-point/0.5). Membership is NEVER touched — eff(±160 ns) is
   bit-identical by construction and by measurement.
2. **Per-group timing uncertainty** (the v1 confidence foundation): the
   same quadratic gives the local χ² curvature `a`, and Δχ²=1 yields
   **σ_t = 1/√a ticks** per group. Delivered per hit as
   `hit_t0_sigma` (NaN = no quadratic fit; conservative fallback), and
   summarized per event in `row["phase3"]["subtick"]`
   (`sigt_med_ns` / `sigt_p90_ns`).
3. No calibration constants: the global bias (~+9 ns, template-shape
   systematic) is left in place — absolute time is not needed.

## Guards (all conservative; centered-window design)

- The 9-point window is CENTERED on the chain time (itself the full
  integer scan's argmin), so no pre-scan exists to edge out of.
- Non-convex fit or vertex beyond ±0.75 ticks → group keeps its chain
  time (counted `n_nonconvex` / `n_range`).
- Movement bounded to ±0.75 ticks; a correct hit cannot leave ±10.

## Measured — CERTIFIED 2026-09-02 (126 events, tag CLMatching_v0.9)

- Headline: **NS 96.50 / all-TPC 95.65 / LE 59.21** — identical to v0.8.9
  (eff invariant by construction and by measurement; 113-ev cert 0 failures,
  scan workload bit-identical).
- Resolution on correctly-assigned core hits (|Δ| ≤ 5 ticks, E-weighted):
  per-hit σ 9.20 → **8.58 ns**; per-group timing uncertainty σ_t delivered
  (median 0.54 ns, p90 ~7 ns).
- Stage E production form: SUM-mode amplitudes (members' stage-1
  amplitudes summed on support channels — measured equal to the
  superposition-exact collective render: 8.58 vs 8.59 ns), centered
  9-shift window, top-30 channels, ≥5 MeV groups. **1.6 s/ev.**
- Latency (file 1, 8 workers × 4 GPUs): 123.7 s/ev/worker =
  **61.8 s/ev/GPU** (v0.8.9-era chain was 78.1).
